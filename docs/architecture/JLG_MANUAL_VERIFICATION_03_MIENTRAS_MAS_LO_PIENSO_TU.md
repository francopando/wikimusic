# JLG Manual Discography Verification 03 — *Mientras más lo pienso... tú*

**Research date:** 2026-08-11  
**Mode:** album-by-album editorial review; production read-only  
**Identity rule:** MusicBrainz identifiers were not used to decide Recording identity.

## Album summary

**Album:** *Mientras más lo pienso... tú*  
**Identity:** third Juan Luis Guerra/4.40 studio album  
**Best-supported original public release:** 1987, Dominican Republic, Karen Records LP KLP 105  
**Original medium:** eight-track, 33⅓ RPM vinyl LP; 33:55 in the Dominican national archive  
**Original track listing:** Guavaberry; Tú; Amor de conuco; No me acostumbro; Me enamoro de ella; ¡Ay! mujer; Amigos; Rock-A-Fiesta.

### Source/date reconciliation

The strongest original-media source is the [Archivo General de la Nación (AGN), Dominican Republic](https://colecciones.agn.gob.do/opac/ficha.php?codopac=OPPUB&informatico=00269530SR). It catalogs the Dominican Karen KLP 105 LP as 1987, supplies the exact side order, and identifies EMCA studio and contributors. A [contemporaneous-format Venezuelan KLP 105 listing](https://www.todocoleccion.net/discos-vinilo/juan-luis-guerra-4-40-mientras-mas-lo-pienso-tu-vinilo-edic-venezuela-1987~x623509669) independently shows the same eight tracks and 1987 issue.

The current [Apple eight-track edition](https://music.apple.com/us/album/mientras-m%C3%A1s-lo-pienso-t%C3%BA/1735315654) and [Spotify catalog](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3nlpTZci9O5W8RsNoNH559) display 1986, with Apple stating `℗ 1986 Karen Records Inc`. The official artist [history page](https://juanluisguerra.com/historia/) and AGN chronology place the album in 1987. The defensible interpretation is **1986 phonogram/recording copyright, 1987 original public LP release**. Mangulina's worldwide 1986 Release should not silently replace the documented Dominican 1987 release assertion.

Apple likewise exposes two materially different catalog editions: an eight-track Karen delivery (`1735315654`, `℗ 1986`) and a seven-track 1995 Karen Publishing delivery (`19512279`) omitting “Rock-A-Fiesta.” Official Apple-preview Chromaprint comparisons for Guavaberry, Tú, Amor de Conuco, and ¡Ay! Mujer scored 0.919–0.970 similarity between these two deliveries. That strongly supports the 1995 edition as the same underlying performances under different mastering/encoding, not rerecordings.

### Independent album/credit evidence

AGN identifies Juan Luis Guerra as singer/arranger; Maridalia Hernández, Roger Zayas-Bazán, and Mariela Mercado as singers; Guarionex Aquino Jr. as percussionist; Kaki Ruiz as trumpeter; José Roberto Olea as trombonist; Manuel Tejada as composer; July Ruiz as composer/arranger; and Salvador Morales as recording engineer. It identifies Karen Records, EMCA studio, and Editora Milvio y Asociados. These are useful album/Recording identity facts, but Mangulina was not edited to add them.

## Duplication pattern compared with *Mudanza y acarreo*

The broad Spanish-reissue duplication pattern from *Mudanza y acarreo* **does not repeat wholesale**. This album has one plausible same-Recording duplicate created within its original album graph: the 230-second Dominican “Amor de conuco” row. A second same-title row, shortened “Tú” on *Antología*, cannot safely be merged because its Track duration conflicts with the platform object attached to it. The other repeated titles are legitimate later recordings/arrangements, not duplication.

The inherited Spanish-1991 “Amigos” duplicate remains a merge candidate from Verification 02 but was not newly created by a *Mientras* edition.

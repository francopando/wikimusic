# JLG Manual Discography Verification 12 — *Todo Tiene Su Hora*

**Research date:** 2026-08-11 · **Mode:** production read-only; independent identity evidence.

## Album evidence

Independent contemporary reporting establishes *Todo Tiene Su Hora* as the next Juan Luis Guerra 4.40 studio album, issued **11 November 2014** by Capitol Latin. The ten-song program is: Cookies & Cream; Tus besos; Canto a Colombia; Todo tiene su hora; Dime Nora mía; Para que sepas; El capitán; Muchachita linda; Todo pasa; De Moca a París. The [contemporary announcement](https://www.diariolibre.com/revista/juan-luis-guerra-presenta-todo-tiene-su-hora-GGDL866541), [retail catalog](https://www.fnac.es/a1072812/Juan-Luis-Guerra-Todo-tiene-su-hora-Disco), and [AllMusic catalog record](https://www.allmusic.com/album/todo-tiene-su-hora-mw0002766095) corroborate date, program and album identity. “De Moca a París” is the original studio duet with Johnny Ventura.

Mangulina contains five album editions: US digital `42048620-...`, US jewel case `d3dea072-...`, country-null `bf5e9766-...`, Spain `acf06f18-...`, and Colombia `ca551d4c-...`. Every edition uses the same ten Recording UUIDs in the same order. The Spain row's `2014-01-01` is placeholder-level date metadata and must not override the independently established 11 November date.

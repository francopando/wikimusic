# JLG Manual Discography Verification 01 — *Soplando*

**Research date:** 2026-08-11  
**Mode:** album-by-album editorial review; production read-only  
**Identity rule:** MusicBrainz identifiers were inspected only as import provenance and were not used to decide whether audio is the same or different.

## Album finding

**Album:** *Soplando*  
**Identity:** first Juan Luis Guerra/4.40 studio album  
**Best-supported original release:** 1984, Dominican Republic, vinyl LP  
**Mangulina Release UUID:** `751bb106-c80b-4562-86f0-0bd2a7260147`  
**Mangulina reissue UUIDs:** `286fc30a-3b20-4538-9f8e-c8dfe09d31c3` (*El Original 4.40*, 1991) and `41c9b094-0d77-4681-a03f-fa5ef3ed1613` (*El original 4: 40*, 2016 digital)  
**Original tracks:** 8  
**Program length:** Dominican national archive: 32:51; current Apple edition: 33 minutes

### Independent sources

1. [Archivo General de la Nación, Dominican Republic — “Álbum musical: 440 soplando”](https://colecciones.agn.gob.do/opac/ficha.php?codopac=OUAUD&idpag=1835051787&informatico=00269532SR). This primary institutional catalog describes a 1984 vinyl, 33 RPM, eight sound recordings, and gives the original side A/side B order exactly: Feliciana; Soplando; Carnaval; Juana Mecho; Jardinera; Sambomba; Loreta; La calle gris.
2. [Apple Music — *El Original 4:40*](https://music.apple.com/gt/album/el-original-4-40/1127625384). The official digital catalog presents the same eight-track program in the same order, dates it 1 January 1984, and states `℗ 1984 M.A.`
3. [Nota Clave, Alfonso Quiñones — 35th-anniversary examination using the original cover/back cover](https://notaclave.com/criticarte-un-homenaje-para-juan-luis-guerra-en-sus-35-anos-de-carrera/). This independently describes the original LP sides, participants, compositions/adaptations, and musical treatment of all eight tracks.
4. [AllMusic — *El Original 4.40*](https://www.allmusic.com/album/el-original-440-mw0000073561). This corroborates the reissue title, eight-track sequence, writers, and approximately matching timings, although its displayed release year is 1990 rather than Mangulina's 1991 edition date.
5. Official Juan Luis Guerra YouTube/label deliveries for individual tracks, cited below. They identify the digital program as *El Original 4:40*, state `℗ 1984 M.A.`, and therefore support reissue of the 1984 recordings rather than a later rerecording.

### Album-level conclusion

Mangulina models this album correctly at the Recording/Track layer. Each of the eight original studio Recordings has three Track appearances: the 1984 *Soplando* release, the 1991 *El Original 4.40* edition, and the 2016 digital *El original 4:40* edition. No second same-title JLG Recording exists for any of the eight songs.

The independent evidence supports the later releases as editions/reissues of the 1984 program. It does not advertise rerecorded vocals, new arrangements, live performances, or remixes. Reported timings differ by approximately 3–6 seconds between the original Mangulina Track, later digital Tracks, and platform display; this is consistent with timing/indexing differences and is not evidence of a new performance. A direct waveform comparison was not necessary to distinguish Mangulina rows because there is only one row per title, but it would be required before asserting bit-identical masters.

## Credits and arrangement observations

Mangulina currently has zero `recording_credits` for all eight rows. Independent album research identifies the founding vocal ensemble as Juan Luis Guerra, Maridalia Hernández, Mariela Mercado, and Roger Zayas-Bazán; Juan Luis Guerra supplied vocal/instrumental arrangements, with specific lead-vocal evidence for Feliciana and Carnaval. These findings should be researched against original liner scans before later insertion.

The album demonstrates why Arrangement and Recording must remain distinct. Traditional songs and adaptations receive identifiable 4.40 arrangements, but the 1991/2016 release appearances do not create new arrangements or Recordings. The evidence supports one 1984 performance per track, distributed through three Releases.

## Dependency and platform observations

Each Recording has exactly one normalized ISRC and one ISRC-source row; none has Recording credits, recording sources, credited-work references, or view events. Seven rows have 3–6 platform links. Sambomba has none, despite appearing on Apple as “Sambombba”; this is a later platform-data repair candidate, not a Recording merge.

No deletion dependency map is needed because this album yields no duplicate Recording candidates.

## Decision and stop

All eight rows should remain. Mangulina already represents later reissues correctly as Tracks pointing to the original Recording rows. `recordings.release_id` on all eight points to the 2016 digital edition rather than the original 1984 Release, so a future governed original-release correction is needed; no production field was changed.

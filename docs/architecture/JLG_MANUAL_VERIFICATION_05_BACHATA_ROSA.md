# JLG Manual Discography Verification 05 — *Bachata Rosa*

**Research date:** 2026-08-11  
**Mode:** album-by-album editorial review; production read-only  
**Identity rule:** MusicBrainz identifiers were not used to decide Recording identity.

## Album summary

**Verified album:** *Bachata Rosa*  
**Original public release:** 11 December 1990, Dominican Republic / international Karen Records release  
**Original Dominican catalog:** Karen Records KLP 136  
**Original format:** ten-track, 12-inch, 33⅓ RPM vinyl LP; 42:32 in the Dominican archive  
**Original side order:** A1 Rosalía; A2 Como abeja al panal; A3 Carta de amor; A4 Estrellitas y duendes; A5 A pedir su mano; B1 La bilirrubina; B2 Burbujas de amor; B3 Bachata rosa; B4 Reforéstame; B5 Acompáñeme civil.

The controlling original-media source is the [Archivo General de la Nación (AGN), Dominican Republic](https://colecciones.agn.gob.do/opac/ficha.php?codopac=OUAUD&idpag=0654351191&informatico=00278508SR&suposi=17). It catalogs the 1990 Dominican Karen KLP 136 vinyl, its exact side order, ten recordings, and 42:32 duration. [Apple](https://music.apple.com/us/album/bachata-rosa/19468976) independently supplies the exact date 11 December 1990, ten tracks, 43 minutes, and `℗ 1990 Karen Publishing Co.` [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/3lRQHKkCpntPvEoqh3C3B0) supplies the same ten-song sequence and 43:13 digital program. No original track substitution was found.

Apple describes four tracks as bachatas and the rest as a mixture including ballad, salsa, merengue, and the African-influenced “A pedir su mano.” Reliable official credits for the 2020 *Privé* rerecording identify Juan Luis Guerra and Janina Rosado as producers and list Guerra, Rosado, Isaías Leclerc, Ernesto Núñez, Roger Zayas, Juan Rizek, Naya Macea, Esar Simó, and Ezequiel Francisco among its performers. Those facts help establish a new recording/arrangement but were not inserted into production.

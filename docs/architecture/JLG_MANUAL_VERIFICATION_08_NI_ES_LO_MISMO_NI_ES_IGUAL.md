# JLG Manual Discography Verification 08 — *Ni Es Lo Mismo Ni Es Igual*

**Research date:** 2026-08-11  
**Mode:** album-by-album editorial review; production read-only  
**Identity rule:** MusicBrainz identifiers were not used to determine Recording identity.

## Album summary

**Canonical title:** *Ni Es Lo Mismo Ni Es Igual*  
**Identity:** eighth Juan Luis Guerra/4.40 studio album and the next studio album after *Fogaraté!*  
**Original public release:** 15 December 1998  
**Phonogram year:** 1998  
**Context:** Dominican artist/production; Karen release, internationally distributed; contemporary Karen CD catalog `930216`; European BMG/Karen catalog `74321 643412`, EAN `0743216434121`  
**Original physical program:** eleven tracks, 37:24, with “Amor de Conuco” identified as the bonus/new pop version.

The [official artist history](https://juanluisguerra.com/historia/) settles the chronology: it calls the 1995 release a *Grandes Éxitos* compilation and identifies *Ni Es Lo Mismo Ni Es Igual* (1998) as Guerra's return after four years. The independent [Amoeba physical CD record](https://www.amoeba.com/ni-es-lo-mismo-ni-es-igual-cd-juan-luis-guerra/albums/779659/) documents 15 December 1998, Karen `930216`, studio CD, 37:24, and the complete eleven-track order. [Charts.org.nz](https://www.charts.nz/showitem.asp?cat=a&interpret=Juan+Luis+Guerra+Y+4.40&titel=Ni+es+lo+mismo+ni+es+igual) independently documents the same date, CD format, BMG/Karen `74321 643412`, and “Amor de conuco (Bonus Track).” [Apple](https://music.apple.com/us/album/ni-es-lo-mismo-ni-es-igual/19469236) confirms ℗ 1998 Karen Publishing Co., but its current 1 November listing is a later ten-track catalog configuration, not the complete original physical program.

**Original sequence:** Mi PC; Vale la pena; La hormiguita; Quisiera; El Niágara en bicicleta; Palomita blanca; Testimonio; Amapola; El primo; Sobremesa; Amor de conuco (bonus/new pop version).

Three Mangulina physical/digital editions preserve all eleven tracks. The ten-track US digital row omits “Amapola,” shifts “El primo” and “Sobremesa” forward, and labels the closer “Amor de conuco (pop version).” This matches the current Apple program. It is an edition omission, not evidence that the remaining songs are different Recordings. Title capitalization, commas, and “Niágara/Niagara” variations are metadata only.

### Credits and arrangement evidence

The physical record credits Juan Luis Guerra as director, producer, arranger and guitarist. Independently documented personnel include Arturo Sandoval (trumpet/flugelhorn), Edwin Bonilla and Luis Enrique (percussion), Juan Valdez (piano), Carlos Álvarez (engineer), Eric Schilling (engineer/mixing), and Scott Kirkland (assistant engineer). These facts were documented, not inserted.

### Song: El Niágara en bicicleta

**Recording UUID:** `2dd6a6a7-81f2-48d1-bd57-bbd75cc36805`

## Arrangement review

## Historical duplication pattern

The broad *Mudanza y acarreo* album-edition duplication does **not** repeat: all four album editions reuse the appropriate canonical UUIDs. The one-Track *The Collection* import pattern repeats five times, including one row incorrectly labelled live. A second known pattern—duplicate imports of one actual tour performance into standard and deluxe live editions—appears for the 2013 “El Niágara en bicicleta.”

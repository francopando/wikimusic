# JLG Manual Discography Verification 02 — *Mudanza y acarreo*

**Research date:** 2026-08-11  
**Mode:** album-by-album editorial review; production read-only  
**Identity rule:** MusicBrainz identifiers were inspected only as import provenance and were not used to decide whether audio is the same or different.

## Album finding

**Album:** *Mudanza y acarreo*  
**Identity:** second Juan Luis Guerra/4.40 studio album  
**Best-supported original release:** 1985, Dominican Republic, Karen Records LP K-91  
**Mangulina Release UUIDs:** `d50289ab-5e15-49dd-9d81-35a6fe9a61d8` (1985 VE), `23d9596d-10b6-4deb-9d0c-48de00846db1` (1985 digital/worldwide), `8eff6b05-921a-44bd-bc3b-ad819af3b394` (1990 US), `cdce6631-0f78-4cef-95a8-a45ed56a554d` (1991 ES), `3010adab-4416-4f1f-a068-42b095f3a723` (1995 US CD; UPC 710793009127)  
**Number of original tracks:** 8  
**Original program:** Por eso ahora; Ella dice; Yo vivo enamorao; Requiem sobre el Jaragua (Le dién dinamita); Si tú te vas; Elena; Santiago en coche; Dame.

### Independent sources

1. [Archivo General de la Nación, Dominican Republic — original-media catalog](https://colecciones.agn.gob.do/opac/fichaimp.php?codopac=OP003&informatico=00269531SR). The institutional record describes an eight-recording 1985 Karen K-91 LP, gives the original A/B sequence, 30:40 program length, EMCA studio, and song-level authorship/adaptation information. It establishes **Dame**, not **Amigos**, as original B4.
2. [Official Juan Luis Guerra history](https://juanluisguerra.com/historia/). The artist chronology lists *Mudanza y Acarreo* in 1985.
3. [Apple Music — *Mudanza y acarreo*](https://music.apple.com/us/album/mudanza-y-acarreo/1737300892). The official Karen digital delivery has eight songs, the Dominican sequence ending in “Dame,” and `℗ 1985 Karen Records Inc.`
4. [Spotify — 1990 Karen Music/EMI Spain edition](https://open.spotify.com/album/2q4SZPhU8wjXj4UhsUEp8I). This official-label delivery has the same first seven tracks but substitutes “Amigos” at track 8; it is expressly a 1990 edition, not evidence that “Amigos” was on the original Dominican LP.
5. [Official JLG YouTube — “Por Eso Ahora”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAT5uMw_-Go). The artist-channel delivery attributes the phonogram to Karen and 1985.
6. [Bull Moose physical-media catalog — 1995 CD](https://www.bullmoose.com/p/1443/juan-luis-guerra-mudanza-y-acarreo). Corroborates UPC 710793009127 and the 1995 CD edition represented in Mangulina.

### Album-level conclusion

The Dominican original and the current Karen digital edition end with **Dame**. Mangulina's Venezuelan 1985, US 1990, and Spanish 1991 programs end with **Amigos**. This is a legitimate regional/reissue **Release-track substitution**, not a version relationship between the two songs. “Amigos” is therefore reviewed as a ninth, album-associated studio Recording but is not counted among the original Dominican LP's eight tracks.

For the first seven songs, Mangulina has a well-connected Recording plus a second row used only by the Spanish 1991 edition. Five duplicate pairs resolve to exactly the same official platform track IDs. “Elena” resolves to the same Spotify and YouTube IDs. “Santiago en coche” resolves to the same Amazon, Apple, Pandora, Spotify, and Tidal IDs. Those seven 1991 rows are same-Recording merge candidates. The shared identifiers are supporting—not dispositive—evidence; the album sequence, label/reissue context, matching performance-length range, and absence of any advertised new performance, arrangement, edit, remix, or rerecording supply the independent discographic context.

“Si tú te vas” is different: its two rows resolve to different platform tracks. Official Apple previews for the album delivery (228.640 s), *Latino! Grandes Éxitos* (228.677 s), and *Juan Luis Guerra y 4.40 Grandes Éxitos* (237.027 s) produced different Chromaprint fingerprints. Because thirty-second platform previews can select different portions and mastering can affect comparison, this does not prove different performances; it does prevent a safe merge without aligned full-track audio. That row remains uncertain.

No later appearance is labelled live, remix, acoustic, alternate take, new arrangement, or rerecording. Apart from the unresolved “Si tú te vas” row, all later Tracks are best supported as reuse/remastering of the original studio performances.

## Later-appearance evidence and limits

Mangulina contains 89 Track appearances across the nine album-associated identity groups (81 on canonical rows and eight on Spanish-1991 secondary rows). Subtracting one first-known appearance for each of the nine recordings yields 80 later appearances under review. Seventy-nine are supported as original-studio reuse/remastered presentation by release chronology, program context, stable platform objects, and the absence of version labelling. One—the secondary Spanish-1991 “Si tú te vas” row—remains uncertain.

This conclusion means “same underlying recorded performance,” not bit-identical file or master. Compilation timing variations, especially the 226–239-second reports for “Si tú te vas,” must not be converted into separate Recording identities without audio evidence. Conversely, none of the researched releases supplies affirmative evidence for a live take, new vocal, new instrumentation, new arrangement, remix, or rerecording.

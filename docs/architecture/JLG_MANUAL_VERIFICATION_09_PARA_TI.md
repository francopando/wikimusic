# JLG Manual Discography Verification 09 — *Para Ti*

**Research date:** 2026-08-11  
**Mode:** sequential manual verification; production read-only  
**Identity rule:** MusicBrainz identifiers were not used as Recording evidence.

## Album evidence

The [official artist history](https://juanluisguerra.com/historia/) identifies *Para Ti* as the next studio album after *Ni Es Lo Mismo Ni Es Igual*, released 31 August 2004, and Guerra's only completely Christian album. [Apple](https://music.apple.com/cd/album/para-ti/1470329634) independently documents eleven songs, approximately 42 minutes, and ℗ 2004 Juan Luis Guerra under exclusive license to UMG. Physical catalog evidence documents Universal/Vene Music CD `27865`. The two Mangulina editions—Spain jewel case `2a760f77-e6d0-4292-b49d-e17e79771ad0` and worldwide/digital `7f654c36-ed16-461a-b619-726af34bd819`—carry the same eleven-track order and reuse the same UUIDs.

**Original program:** Soldado; Eres; Los dinteles; Gloria; Mi padre me ama; Las avispas; Tan solo he venido; Para ti; Extiende tu mano; Aleluya; Canción de sanidad.

Juan Luis Guerra is independently credited as composer, arranger, producer, vocalist and musical director; the album's supported credit context belongs across Work, Arrangement and Recording layers and was not inserted.
